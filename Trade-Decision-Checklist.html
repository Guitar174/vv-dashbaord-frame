Trade Decision Checklist

BEFORE BUYING

All 5 must be YES 0 / 5
1
Market is crashing, or price is at an EMA support level
No clear technical level = no valid entry. Wait.
2
Portfolio has fewer than 5 stocks open
Already at 5? Sell a loser first before opening anything new.
3
Cash stays above 25% after this buy
Each tranche = 50,000 THB max. Never oversize one entry.
4
Position size is within its risk tier limit
Quality (PLTR / AVGO) — max 15% Medium (RKLB) — max 8% Speculative (ONDS) — max 3%
5
This stock is NOT in the same category as one I already hold
Two names in the same theme = one bet, not two. Same category? Add to the existing position instead, or skip.

BEFORE SELLING

All 5 must be YES 0 / 5
1
Price is at a resistance or target level
Selling early cuts winners short. Wait for a real technical reason.
2
I am NOT selling out of fear or impatience
Ask: is the thesis broken — or am I just uncomfortable? If uncomfortable, hold.
3
This stock has no remaining upside potential
Check catalysts, earnings, news. If it can still run — do NOT sell.
4
The overall market is NOT in a broad bullish trend
In a bull run, selling into market strength = leaving money on the table.
5
I have asked AI before making the final decision
Talk through the trade. If AI confirms the exit, then sell. Never decide alone.

AFTER BUYING

Review these every day 0 / 5
1
Stop-loss is set and has NOT been moved lower
Moving the stop down = breaking the rule. Don't do it.
2
If down −15%, I have reviewed the thesis
Still valid? Hold. Thesis broken? Cut at least half now.
3
If down −20%, I have cut at least half
A −50% loss needs a +100% gain to break even. Cut early, preserve capital.
4
If at a peak, I have taken partial profits
Sell half first, then restore up. Ask AI before deciding on the rest.
5
I did NOT sell just because the price rose a little
Fear-based selling cuts winners short. Let quality run.

One unchecked box means no trade. Tap a row (or press Space / Enter) to check it; the page remembers your state.
