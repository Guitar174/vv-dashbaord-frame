Day 1 — Arrive in Fukuoka

  • Fly into Fukuoka Airport (5 min subway to Hakata Station)
  • Walk Hakata / Tenjin, get oriented
  • Evening: Yatai food stalls at Nakasu or Tenjin — open-air riverside stalls, tonkotsu ramen, yakitori. Arrive ~18:30. Cash only.
  • Optional: Shochu tasting at Sumiyoshi Liquor Store (Hakata Station) — standing bar, sample Kyushu shochu by the glass
  • Free Kushida Shrine — Fukuoka's oldest shrine (founded 757 AD), free entry. Home to a towering Hakata Gion Yamakasa float on permanent display.

Day 2 — Kumamoto

  • 🚄 Shinkansen Hakata → Kumamoto (33 min, ~¥5,300)
  • Pick up rental car at Kumamoto Station
  • Kumamoto Castle — impressive stone walls, earthquake reconstruction. ~1.5 hrs.
  • Kamishikimi Kumanoimasu Shrine (~1 hr drive) — 260 moss-covered stone steps through a cedar forest leading to a sacred rock arch. Late afternoon light through the canopy is the photo window. Wear grippy shoes.
  • Free Ninomaru Park — walk the outer castle grounds and photograph the keep and stone walls without paying inner-castle admission. Kamishikimi Shrine itself is also free to enter.

Day 3 — Takachiho ⭐

  • Leave by 09:00 (the one early day — gorge boat has a narrow light window)
  • Drive ~1.5–2 hrs to Takachiho
  • 🚣 Takachiho Gorge boat ride (aim for 10:30–11:30) — row through basalt gorge, Manai Falls lit by sunlight. Water glows emerald green. After ~14:00 the gorge goes into shadow.
  • Walk the gorge rim trail
  • Amano-Iwato Shrine (optional, ~15 min drive) — mythological cave of the sun goddess. Ask at shrine office for guided viewing (free). ~30 min.
  • Takachiho Kagura (optional, 20:00 if staying overnight) — traditional Shinto dance at the shrine. ¥1,000. Living ritual, not a tourist performance.
  • Free Takachiho Shrine (town center) — 800-year-old cedar trees, free to wander. Also: the gorge rim trail and viewpoints cost nothing — only the boat ride itself is paid.

Day 4 — Aso + Kurokawa Onsen ⭐

Morning / Afternoon — Aso Caldera

  • Drive north from Takachiho (~1–1.5 hrs)
  • Kusasenri Observatory & Grasslands — vast volcanic plateau, horses, smoking crater. Feels like Iceland.
    • Optional: Horseback riding (~5 min guided ride, ~¥1,500, cash). You're on a horse on a volcano.
  • Milk Road drive — scenic caldera rim road. The drive itself is the attraction.
  • Daikanbō viewpoint (~15:00–16:00) — 360° panorama of the Aso Five Peaks at golden hour. Bring a jacket.
  • Kokonoe "Yume" Suspension Bridge (between Aso and Kurokawa, 15-min detour) — 173m above a gorge, waterfall views. (¥500 crossing fee)
  • Free Kusasenri grassland walk, the Milk Road drive, and Daikanbō viewpoint all cost nothing — only the horseback ride and the Aso Volcano Museum building charge admission.

Evening — Kurokawa Onsen

Kurokawa Onsen — Wikimedia Commons
  • Check in to ryokan
  • Buy Nyūtō Tegata (¥1,500) — wooden pass for 3 different outdoor baths across the village
  • Onsen hop in yukata, walk lantern-lit stone streets
  • Kaiseki dinner at your ryokan (included in stay)
  • Stargazing from rotenburo — Kurokawa has strict rules against bright lighting. On a clear night, you'll see stars from the outdoor bath.
  • Free Walking the lantern-lit village streets in yukata — no charge just to wander; only the onsen tegata pass and dinner cost extra.

Day 5 — Kurokawa → Fukuoka (Buffer Day)

  • Morning soak + ryokan breakfast. No rush.
  • Drive back to Kumamoto (~1.5 hrs)
  • Return rental car at Kumamoto Station
  • 🚄 Shinkansen Kumamoto → Hakata (33 min, ~¥5,300)
  • Free afternoon/evening in Fukuoka — rest, revisit yatai, walk Ohori Park, or do nothing
  • Free Ohori Park lakeside loop — full circuit is free, no admission, and doubles as sakura viewing if blooming.

Day 6 — Day Trip

Default: Nagasaki 🏙️

Recommended — culturally unique, strong regardless of weather

  • 🚄 Shinkansen to Nagasaki (~1.5 hrs)
  • Peace Park + Atomic Bomb Museum — heavy but important
  • Glover Garden — hilltop harbour district from the Dutch/Portuguese trade era
  • Mt. Inasa ropeway for sunset → night view (rated top 3 city night views in the world)
  • Shinkansen back
  • Free Hike the Mt. Inasa walking trail instead of the ropeway (~40–60 min) — same night view, ¥0.
  • Free Peace Park — free entry (only the adjacent Atomic Bomb Museum charges admission).
