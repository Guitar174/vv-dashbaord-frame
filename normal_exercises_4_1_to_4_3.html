How to read the pictures: the curve is the standard normal N(0,1). The orange area is the region the question asks about, and its size is the answer. The Z-table gives the area to the LEFT of a Z-value.
Three tricks: ① Left tail → read the table directly. ② Right tail → 1 − (table value). ③ Between two Z-values → big left area − small left area.
